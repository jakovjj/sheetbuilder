Smart DTF Gang Sheet Builder — Free Online Tool

How to use SheetBuilder

Upload your images, set each image size (mm) and copies, then choose your paper size and margins. When you’re ready, click Generate Layout and then Export to PDF. Feel free to contact jakov@jandric.com for support or feature requests.

SheetBuilder automatically packs your DTF designs onto a single gang sheet using an efficient bin-packing algorithm — no wasted film, no manual layout. Works with any sheet width or roll size. Export to high-resolution PDF or PNG (up to 1200 DPI) ready for your DTF printer. Free, open source, no signup required. Your images never leave your browser.

How it works?

Upload as many images as you want. Each image shows a width and height field you can edit freely. Changing either dimension will automatically adjust the other to keep the original aspect ratio. You can also select multiple images at once, then edit any field to update all selected images at the same time.
