SheetBuilder for Print-and-Play (PnP) Games

SheetBuilder is widely used by the tabletop and board game community as a free print-and-play layout tool. Whether you're playtesting a prototype or printing a final PnP release, the auto-packing engine handles all the tedious layout work for you.

Print-and-play designers and players need to fit as many cards, tiles, and tokens as possible onto a single sheet to minimize paper and ink usage. SheetBuilder's smart bin-packing algorithm does exactly that: upload your card faces as PNG images, set each one to standard card dimensions (e.g., 63 x 88 mm for poker-size cards or 44 x 67 mm for mini cards), enter how many copies you need, and hit Generate Layout. The tool arranges everything automatically with configurable spacing between cards so they're easy to cut.

You can mix card sizes on the same sheet, separate different component types onto distinct pages using the Separate by Image Size option, and export the finished layout as a print-ready PDF or high-resolution PNG. Everything runs in your browser with no file uploads, which is important when working with unreleased or proprietary PnP game art.

Common PnP use cases

  • Card games: Pack poker-size, tarot-size, or mini cards onto A4, Letter, or custom sheet sizes for home printing.
  • Tile-based games: Arrange square or rectangular tiles at any scale, with equal spacing for clean guillotine cuts.
  • Prototype testing: Quickly iterate on new card designs by uploading updated PNGs and regenerating the layout in seconds.
  • Token sheets: Fit dozens of small circular or square tokens onto a single page using rotation to maximize coverage.
  • Mixed components: Combine cards, tiles, and reference sheets on separate pages in one export run using the page-separation feature.
  • Publisher print files: Generate production-ready PDFs at 300 DPI or higher for sending to a professional print shop or POD service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a DTF gang sheet?

A DTF (Direct-to-Film) gang sheet is a single sheet or roll of transfer film that holds multiple designs packed together as efficiently as possible. Printing multiple transfers at once reduces film waste and lowers the cost per transfer. Gang sheets are standard practice for DTF print shops, custom apparel decorators, and heat-transfer resellers.

Is SheetBuilder free to use?

Yes. SheetBuilder is completely free, with no account, no subscription, and no watermark. It's also open source. You can use it as many times as you like to build gang sheets for personal or commercial DTF printing work. There's no hidden cost or trial limit.

How is SheetBuilder different from other free DTF gang sheet makers?

Most free gang sheet builders either upload your files to a server, add watermarks, or limit how many designs you can use. SheetBuilder runs entirely in your browser, so nothing is uploaded anywhere. There are no limits on designs, copies, or exports. The smart bin-packing algorithm with rotation support is the same approach used by professional layout software.

Does SheetBuilder upload my images to a server?

No. SheetBuilder processes everything locally in your browser. Your images, artwork, and designs never leave your device: no server uploads, no cloud storage, no privacy risk. This makes it ideal for printing shops that work with proprietary or licensed artwork.

What file formats are supported for import and export?

SheetBuilder accepts JPG, PNG, GIF, and WEBP image files. PNG with transparency is the best format for DTF transfers. For export, you can save your gang sheet as a high-resolution PDF (perfect, high, medium, or low compression) or as a PNG at 72, 150, 300, 600, or 1200 DPI. Most DTF RIP software and online print shops accept both formats.

Can I use SheetBuilder for DTF roll printing?

Yes. Set a custom paper width to match your roll (e.g., 600 mm for a 24" roll) and leave the height open-ended. Use the Fill Until Pages option to repeat the layout automatically until a target page count is reached, ideal for producing long roll-length DTF gang sheets with minimal waste.

What is the smart layout algorithm?

SheetBuilder uses a rectangle bin-packing algorithm that places each design in the optimal position on the sheet. It tries both original and rotated orientations for every image to find the arrangement with the least wasted space. This is the same principle behind professional gang sheet generators used by large-format DTF printers and print-and-play layout tools.

Can I set different sizes and copy counts for each design?

Yes. Each image can have its own width, height (locked to aspect ratio), and copy count. You can also select multiple images at once and bulk-edit their size. This makes it easy to build gang sheets that mix large chest prints, small pocket logos, and sleeve designs, or PnP sheets that mix different card sizes all in one session.

Can I use SheetBuilder for print-and-play card games?

Yes. SheetBuilder is a popular free print-and-play layout tool for tabletop game designers and players. Upload your card art as PNG files, set standard card dimensions (poker size is 63 x 88 mm, mini cards are 44 x 67 mm), set your copy count, and generate a packed layout ready to print on A4 or Letter paper. The bleed and spacing controls make it easy to produce clean, cuttable PnP sheets.
